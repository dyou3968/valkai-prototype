How we handle your data

Standing guarantees on every plan — enforced everywhere.
✓Never used for training. Your documents do not train any model — ours or our providers'. Zero-retention inference terms are in place with all model vendors.
✓Encrypted and isolated. Encrypted in transit and at rest; your workspace index is never blended with another customer's.
✓Delete means delete. Removing a document purges the file, its index entries, and derived artifacts within minutes.
